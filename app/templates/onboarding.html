📌 How to reach your incubator from now on
  1. Reconnect your phone to your home Wi-Fi , the {{ ap_ssid }} setup network is about to disappear.
  2. Open your browser (Safari or Chrome).
  3. Type this address into the address bar, tap it below to select it:

Once it loads, bookmark it or use your browser menu to Add to Home Screen, then your incubator is one tap away, just like an app.

Android tip: if that address won't load, some Android phones can't use .local names, open your Wi-Fi router's app or device list, find your incubator's name, and use its IP address (like http://192.168.1.23:8000) instead.

Safety net: if the address doesn't work after a couple of minutes, the Wi-Fi join probably failed, your incubator will automatically turn its {{ ap_ssid }} setup network back on. Reconnect to it and we'll fix the Wi-Fi together; everything else stays saved.
